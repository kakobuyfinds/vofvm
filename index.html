What is a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet?

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds before researching an order through a shopping agent. Useful entries identify the product, source listing, available options and reference price.

This page uses a selection from HipobuyFind. Inclusion does not confirm that LoveGoBuy accepts an item or supports its source link. Check the original listing and your chosen platform before placing an order.

From browsing to a checked shortlist

How to use this LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

  1. Start with the product type.

    Filter for shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, jackets or bags. Search a descriptive term or item number to locate a specific catalog entry.

  2. Open the source listing.

    Record the product ID and locate the original seller link. Confirm that the listing still matches the image and description you saved.

  3. Choose the exact option.

    Compare measurements, color, material and version. A photograph may show a different option from the one selected by default.

  4. Check the buying platform.

    If using LoveGoBuy, confirm link support and item eligibility in your account. Review the current order summary and return conditions before payment.

  5. Review before international shipment.

    Where warehouse inspection is available, compare the received item with your chosen option. Resolve discrepancies and confirm the parcel quote before approving dispatch.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the buttons take me?

The “LoveGoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://hipobuyfind.com. Individual product links open their matching HipobuyFind detail pages. The buttons do not add a product to a cart or complete a purchase.

Is this an official LoveGoBuy website?

No. This is an independent product discovery page. It does not claim an official LoveGoBuy partnership and does not collect payment or manage orders.

Are these real products from HipobuyFind?

The six entries were taken from the public HipobuyFind catalog on 19 November 2026. Each includes the source item ID, original image URL and matching detail-page link. This is a selected static import, not a complete or continuously synchronized catalog.

Are the prices live and is stock guaranteed?

No. Prices are reference values from the dated snapshot. Sellers may change prices, withdraw listings or charge different amounts for different options. Confirm the current listing before paying.

Can I order every item through LoveGoBuy?

That has not been verified. Find the original marketplace listing and check that your chosen platform accepts its link, product type and selected option before proceeding.

What is the difference between product images and QC photos?

Product images illustrate the catalog listing. Quality-control or inspection photos document a particular received item. This page displays source catalog images and does not label them as verified warehouse QC.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The displayed reference prices are item prices. Use a current platform quote for international shipping and account for domestic delivery, fees and applicable taxes when comparing your total.

Do I need Excel or a Google account?

No. The catalog and guide are readable directly in your browser. JavaScript adds local search, category filtering, sorting and the budget calculation.
